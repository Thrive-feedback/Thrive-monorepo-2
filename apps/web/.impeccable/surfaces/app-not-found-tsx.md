# Not found (404)

Scope: `app/not-found.tsx`, every URL nothing lives at, signed in or out. Mode: Persuade (the visitor's
one act is to get somewhere real). Code-led: no image generation on this machine.

Audience: a Member who followed an old link or mistyped one, or a signed-out visitor.
Job: understand nothing is here, then go somewhere real in one press.
Constraints: no navbar (only the root layout stands); never fails itself, so a session it cannot
read counts as signed out; reduced motion skips the flight.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The 404 is a letter Thrive could not deliver: an envelope addressed to the path the visitor
asked for, cancelled ADDRESS UNKNOWN by Thrive's own postmark, which the visitor re-addresses to a
real page. Refuses the giant "404" numeral over an apology and a button.

OWN-WORLD: Thrive's postcard stock (raised surface, dot grid, cream tint), worn blue rubber-stamp
ink from the Postmark atom, Caveat handwriting in the secondary orange for the person's voice, the
Cooper display face for the heading, the brand purple primary button, the signed-out orb ground.

STORY: The visitor reads their own path written on an envelope and struck out, understands the
page is not here, and picks where the letter should go instead.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thrive mark top-left. A display heading, then the front of an envelope, centred,
about 34rem wide at 3:2, tilted a degree: From Thrive in the sender's corner top left, a perforated
purple stamp (wordmark reversed, 404 denomination) top right, cancelled by the ADDRESS UNKNOWN ·
NO. 404 postmark whose waves run across it; the address block lower-middle, a handwritten aside
with an arrow down to "To" and the path written by hand on its rule and crossed out. Under it
"re-address it to…" and the primary action, Home (or Thrive when signed out), then the other
destinations and Go back as quiet links in one line.

FORM: Re-addressed envelope, 4th of 7 on the ordered list; seed:f5ab07a5ee24eed6. Signature
interaction: picking a destination writes it on the envelope by hand, then the envelope flies off
and the page follows. Motion grammar: one arrival moment, the postmark strike with the cross-out chained into it, and
one flight on departure; the handwritten notes are static.
